The requester asked for the cards of a password reset feature, and for the slices to be proposed. The skill cuts a feature into the smallest slices a person can see working on the product, by behaviour and never by component or team.

PASS if the reply proposes slices in an order, and each one is something a person can do or see on the product, in any wording and language.

FAIL if the reply proposes no slices, or a slice is a component or a layer, such as "backend endpoint", "email service" or "database table", rather than something a person can see working.
